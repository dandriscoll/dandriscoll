## What is it?

Tail Number is an offline aircraft registration database for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. Look up any aircraft registered with the FAA or Transport Canada — even without cell service. Track which aircraft you've encountered while flying and export your data via CSV.

## Key Features

- **Fully offline** — The entire FAA and Transport Canada registration database ships with the app, no internet required.
- **Aircraft encounter tracking** — Log and track which aircraft you've seen or flown.
- **CSV import/export** — Bring in your own data or export your encounters for use elsewhere.
- **Privacy-first** — No data collection whatsoever.
