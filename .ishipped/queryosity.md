## What is it?

Queryosity is like a private Twitter feed for your brain. Jot down thoughts, ideas, and notes throughout the day — quick, low-friction, just like posting a tweet. Over time those entries build up into a personal knowledge base that gets organized by topic and indexed with semantic search. When you're ready to do something with all of it, start a chat and an LLM draws on your accumulated thoughts as context, giving you responses grounded in what you've actually been thinking about.

## Key Features

- **Stream-of-thought capture** — Post quick entries like a private feed, no formatting or structure required
- **Topic-based organization** — Tag and categorize entries so they cluster into useful context over time
- **AI chat grounded in your notes** — Start a conversation and the LLM pulls in your most relevant thoughts via semantic search
- **Searchable knowledge base** — Azure AI Search indexes everything so the right context surfaces automatically
- **Private by design** — User-scoped data isolation with Azure Managed Identity authentication
